About This Testbed

This Hurricane & Typhoon Historical Testbed is a research-style interface for exploring major tropical cyclones across all ocean basins. It draws from publicly available best-track data concepts (HURDAT-style), historical records, and published fatality/intensity analyses.

The loading sequence is styled after the classic Roblox experience using the BobloxClassic font (same one used in ArmorTest). Everything after that uses a clean professional typeface and interface suitable for serious meteorological review.

Coverage notes: The catalog prioritizes historically significant storms — the deadliest, strongest, costliest, and culturally notable events from the Atlantic, Eastern & Western Pacific, North Indian Ocean, and Southern Hemisphere. It is not an exhaustive list of every named or unnamed system (that database exceeds 15,000 entries).

Data sources conceptually include NOAA NHC HURDAT2, JTWC best tracks, historical reanalyses, and published fatality compilations. Individual storm details may reflect best available estimates; older events often carry large uncertainty ranges.

Built for exploration, education, and testing of tropical cyclone historical knowledge.
